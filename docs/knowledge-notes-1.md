**1. Domain Overview**

The Supply Chain & Logistics domain manages the flow of goods, information, and
finances from raw material sourcing to final delivery. It covers procurement,
manufacturing, warehousing, transportation, and distribution. Business analysts work on
ERP/SCM implementations, demand forecasting, inventory optimization, warehouse
automation, and logistics visibility platforms.

**2. Key Stakeholders**​


| Key Stakeholders | Stakeholder Role / Interest |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Suppliers & Vendors** | Provide raw materials, goods, services |
| **Manufacturers** | Convert raw materials into finished goods |
| **Logistics Providers (3PL/4PL)** | Handle warehousing, transportation, last-mile delivery |
| **Distributors & Wholesalers** | Move goods across regions/channels |
| **Retailers & E-commerce** | Sell products to end customers |
| **Customers (B2B/B2C)** | Expect timely, accurate deliveries |
| **Regulators & Customs** | Enforce trade laws, tariffs, compliance |
| **Finance Teams** | Manage procurement spend, freight costs |
| **IT/SCM Vendors** | Provide ERP, WMS, TMS, SCM, and analytics tools |


**3. Common Processes**

* Procurement & Sourcing: Vendor selection, purchase orders, supplier contracts.
* Production Planning & Scheduling: Forecasting demand, managing production
runs.
* Inventory Management: Stock control, reorder levels, safety stock optimization.
* Warehousing & Fulfillment: Receiving, picking, packing, storage, distribution.
* Transportation Management: Route planning, carrier selection, shipment
tracking.
*​ Last-Mile Delivery: Ensuring on-time delivery to end customers.
* Reverse Logistics: Returns, recycling, and recalls.
* Customs & Trade Compliance: Duties, tariffs, cross-border clearance.
* Supply Chain Finance: Vendor payments, freight settlements.

**4. Key Systems/Applications**

* ERP Systems: SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics (end-to-end supply chain
management).
* WMS (Warehouse Management Systems): Manage storage, picking, inventory
accuracy.
* TMS (Transportation Management Systems): Optimize routing, carrier
management.
* SCM Platforms: End-to-end visibility (Kinaxis, Blue Yonder, Manhattan
Associates).
* Order Management Systems (OMS): Handle multi-channel orders.
* Supplier Portals: Vendor collaboration, procurement automation.
* IoT/Telematics: Real-time tracking of goods & vehicles.
* Analytics & Demand Forecasting Tools: AI/ML-driven predictions.

**5. Regulatory/Compliance Factors**

*​ Customs & Trade Laws: Import/export duties, tariffs, documentation.
* Incoterms: Standardized international shipping terms.
* Environmental Regulations: Emission standards, green logistics compliance.
* Occupational Safety: OSHA, warehouse safety protocols.
* Data Privacy: GDPR/CCPA in customer and supplier data.
* Food/Pharma Supply Chains: FDA/EMA compliance for cold chain integrity.

**6. Common Pain Points**

* Supply chain disruptions (pandemics, geopolitical issues).
* Forecasting inaccuracies leading to stockouts/overstocking.
* High logistics costs, especially in last-mile delivery.
* Lack of real-time visibility across suppliers & carriers.
* Customs delays and regulatory complexity.
* Legacy ERP/WMS systems with poor integration.
* Rising sustainability and carbon compliance requirements.

**7. Opportunities for BAs**

* Map & redesign end-to-end procurement-to-delivery workflows.
* Implement real-time visibility platforms for shipments.
* Automate demand forecasting & inventory replenishment.
* Optimize last-mile delivery with route optimization tools.
* Enable supplier collaboration portals for procurement.
* Define KPIs for cost-to-serve, order fill rate, and on-time delivery.
* Support sustainability reporting & green logistics initiatives.

**8. Commonly Used Terminology (Quick Glossary)**

* 3PL/4PL: Third/Fourth Party Logistics providers.
* Lead Time: Time from order placement to delivery.
* Fill Rate: % of customer demand met without stockouts.
* Safety Stock: Extra inventory kept to prevent shortages.
* SKU: Stock Keeping Unit, unique identifier for a product.
* Incoterms: International commercial terms defining buyer/seller responsibilities.
* Reverse Logistics: Handling returns, recycling, disposal.
* Cold Chain: Temperature-controlled supply chain for perishable goods.

**9. Emerging Trends**

* AI/ML in Demand Forecasting: Improved accuracy in planning.
* Blockchain for Supply Chain Transparency: Tamper-proof traceability.
* IoT & Telematics: Real-time asset and fleet monitoring.
* Green Logistics: Electric fleets, carbon-neutral delivery models.
* Digital Twins: Virtual replicas of supply chains for simulation.
* Hyperautomation in Warehousing: Robotics, drones, automated picking
systems.
* Resilient Supply Chains: Diversification of suppliers, nearshoring strategies.
